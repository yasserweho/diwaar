A "portion" is one floor of a house that the owner rents out separately: the **upper portion** (first floor, often with its own staircase) or the **lower portion** (ground floor, usually with the lawn or car porch). For many families and working couples in Islamabad, a portion is the middle ground between a small flat and a full house: more space than an apartment, a lower rent than a whole house, and a residential street instead of a tower block.

When people in Pakistan search Google for this, they don't just type "portion for rent". They type a sector: *portion for rent in G-13*, *upper portion for rent in D-12*, *portion for rent in I-10*. This guide follows the same logic.

## Upper portion or lower portion: which one?

| | Upper portion | Lower portion |
|---|---|---|
| Typical pluses | More privacy, less street noise, often a separate entrance | Lawn/porch access, no stairs (good for elderly parents and small children) |
| Typical minuses | Stairs, roof heat in summer, water pressure on upper floors | Less privacy, owner's family may be upstairs |
| Ask the owner | Is there a separate gate and staircase? Separate meters? Roof access? | Who uses the lawn and car porch? Is the upper floor occupied? |

Neither is better for everyone. Decide on the three things that matter most to you (privacy, parking, stairs) before you start viewing.

## Sector notes: where people search most

The sectors below appear most often in Pakistani Google searches for portions in Islamabad (autocomplete, November 2026). We don't publish average rents here, because rents change street by street and month by month; compare the live ads instead.

### G-13
One of the most searched sectors for portions and upper portions. Popular with families and with people who work in the G-, H- and I-sector belt and around Kashmir Highway. Ask about water supply (some streets rely on boring or tankers) and the walking distance to the nearest markaz.

### G-11 and G-10
Established, central sectors with mature markets and schools nearby. Portions here tend to be older houses, so check the condition of washrooms, kitchen and wiring, and ask when the house was last renovated.

### I-10 and I-8
Close to the I-9 industrial area and the Faizabad/IJP Road side, which makes them practical for people who commute to Rawalpindi. I-8 is generally the more expensive of the two. Check the traffic route to your office at peak hours, not on a Sunday.

### D-12
A newer, quieter sector near the Margalla foothills, with mostly newer houses. Upper portions here are frequently searched. Public transport options are thinner than in the G-sectors, so it suits people with their own car.

### E-11
Mixed sector with apartments and houses; both portions and flats are common. Ask whether the plot is in the CDA sector or in one of the private housing societies inside E-11. CDA keeps a public status list of approved private schemes in E-11 and Zone 2/5 (see Sources).

G-9, G-8, F-10, F-11, B-17, Bahria Town and DHA Islamabad, Gulberg Greens. diwaar.com has a page for each area under [Locations → Islamabad](/locations/islamabad).

## Before you pay anything: 8 checks

1. **See the portion in person.** Never pay a "booking" or "token" amount for a portion you have only seen in photos or on WhatsApp.
2. **Meet the owner or an authorised person.** Ask to see the owner's CNIC and a utility bill in the owner's name for that address.
3. **Separate meters?** Ask whether electricity, gas and water are separate for each portion, or shared and split. Shared meters are a common source of disputes.
4. **Write the rent agreement before moving in.** Monthly rent, security deposit, notice period, annual increase, who pays which bill, and the condition of the portion at handover.
5. **Photograph the portion on the day you move in**, especially walls, fittings and fixtures, and keep the photos with your agreement.
6. **Pay by bank transfer, not cash**, and keep the receipt or screenshot every month.
7. **Register the tenancy with Islamabad Police.** ICT Police say tenant registration is mandatory for every tenant in Islamabad. The tenant or the owner can apply online or at a Police Khidmat Markaz with both CNICs and the rent agreement (check the ICT Police page for the current fee and documents).
8. **Know where disputes go.** Rent disputes in Islamabad are heard by the Rent Controller under the Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance, 2001.

## FAQ

**Is tenant registration really required in Islamabad?**
ICT Police describe it as mandatory for all tenants in Islamabad Capital Territory. Either the owner or the tenant can apply. Check the ICT Police page for the current fee and documents.

**What is the difference between a portion and a flat?**
A flat is a unit in an apartment building. A portion is a floor of an ordinary house that the owner rents out separately.

**How much security deposit is normal?**
It's whatever the owner and tenant agree in writing. Make sure the agreement says when and how the deposit will be returned.

**Can bachelors rent a portion?**
Some owners rent only to families. Good ads say so up front; if an ad doesn't, ask before you visit.
