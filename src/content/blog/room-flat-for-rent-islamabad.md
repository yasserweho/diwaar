## Room, shared flat, studio or hostel?

| Option | Suits | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Single room in a house | Students and single professionals on a budget | House rules (guests, timings), shared kitchen and washroom |
| Shared flat | Friends or colleagues splitting rent | Whose name is on the agreement; what happens if one person leaves |
| Studio / 1-bed flat | Working professionals, couples | Maintenance charges, lift and backup power in towers |
| Hostel | First months in the city, students | What is included (meals, laundry, Wi-Fi), notice period |

## 7 questions to ask before you agree

1. **Furnished means what, exactly?** Get a written list: bed, mattress, wardrobe, fridge, AC, geyser, internet.
2. **Which bills are included?** Electricity is the big one in summer. Is it a separate meter, a sub-meter, or a fixed share?
3. **Backup power and water?** Ask about UPS/generator and whether water comes from the supply line, a bore, or tankers.
4. **What is the notice period**, and how is the deposit returned?
5. **Who else lives here?** For rooms in a house, meet the owner's family or the other tenants.
6. **Can I see the owner's CNIC and a utility bill for this address?**
7. **Will you register the tenancy with the police?** In Islamabad, ICT Police say tenant registration is mandatory for every tenant. Bring your CNIC; the owner can also apply.

## How to avoid the most common scam

The classic pattern: a very good flat at a very low rent, photos that look like a hotel, an "owner" who is abroad or "can't show it today", and a request to send an advance by bank or mobile wallet to "hold" it.

- **Don't pay anything before you have seen the place and met the owner or a person they've authorised in writing.**
- Reverse-search the photos (Google Lens). Stolen photos often appear on other sites.
- If the rent is far below similar ads in the same sector, ask why.

## FAQ

**Can I rent a room per day in Islamabad?**
People do search for "room for rent in Islamabad per day", but daily rentals are usually guest houses or short-stay apartments, not monthly tenancies. Check the property is allowed to operate as a guest house.

**Do students need a guarantor?**
Some owners ask for a parent's CNIC or a guarantor. It's normal to put that in the rent agreement.

**Is a written agreement necessary for a single room?**
Yes. A one-page agreement listing the rent, deposit, notice period and bills prevents most disputes, and police registration usually asks for it.
