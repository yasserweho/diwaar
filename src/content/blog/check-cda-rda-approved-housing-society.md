Plot files in unapproved schemes are one of the most expensive mistakes a buyer can make in the Islamabad–Rawalpindi region. Both the **Capital Development Authority (CDA)** in Islamabad and the **Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA)** publish lists you can check yourself, for free, before paying anything. This guide shows where they are and how to read them.

> **Disclaimer:** diwaar.com does not certify housing schemes. Always confirm the current status with the authority itself.

---

## First: LOP vs NOC

In Islamabad, CDA approves private housing schemes in **two tiers**:

1. **Layout Plan (LOP) approval:** the plan of the scheme is approved.
2. **No Objection Certificate (NOC):** issued after further formalities. CDA's own notice says sponsors can **start development and sale of plots only after obtaining the NOC**.

CDA also issues a **Completion Certificate** once development is finished according to the approved plan.

So "LOP approved" is **not** the same as "approved for sale". Ask the seller which stage the scheme has reached, then check it yourself.

---

## Islamabad (CDA): 4 steps

1. **Open CDA's housing schemes page** and find the scheme's name and zone: https://www.cda.gov.pk/housing-schemes
2. **Check the illegal schemes list.** CDA publishes an alphabetical list of schemes where "plots are being sold/marketed" without any CDA approval or NOC: https://www.cda.gov.pk/illegal-housing-schemes
3. **Read the status table** for approved private schemes in Zone 2, Zone 5 and E-11. It shows the LOP approval date, whether an NOC was issued, and remarks such as *cancelled*: https://www.cda.gov.pk/Assets/pdf/status_of_housing_socities.pdf
4. **Verify the specific property.** CDA runs an online Property Verification service: https://www.cda.gov.pk/public/onlinePropertyVerification. It also advises buyers to check for plots sold beyond the approved area, changes to the approved layout, amenity plots converted to residential use, and plots still **mortgaged with CDA**.

## Rawalpindi (RDA): 3 steps

1. **Approved schemes:** https://rda.gop.pk/ahs/
2. **Illegal / unauthorised schemes:** https://rda.gop.pk/ihs/
3. Match the **exact scheme name, mouza (revenue estate) and location**. Many schemes have similar names. If a seller says a scheme is "under process" or "file submitted", treat it as **not** approved until it appears on the approved list.

---

## Red flags

- The scheme name is close to, but not exactly the same as, an approved scheme.
- The seller says "NOC is coming soon" but asks for full payment now.
- Big "balloting" or "launch" discounts with pressure to decide today.
- No physical site office, or the site office can't show the NOC letter.
- Payment requested to a personal account instead of the society's official account.

## What to ask for before you pay

- A copy of the **NOC letter** (and LOP) with its number and date. Then check it against the authority's list.
- The **allotment letter / file** in the seller's name, and the society's **transfer** procedure and fee.
- The **site plan** showing your plot's location in the approved layout.

---

## FAQ

**Is a society safe if it appears on the approved list?**
Approval lowers the risk; it doesn't remove it. You still need to verify the individual plot, its ownership and dues.

**Where do I complain about an illegal scheme in Islamabad?**
Contact CDA directly. Its Housing Schemes and Online Property Verification pages (see Sources) list the offices that deal with private schemes.

**Do these lists cover DHA and Bahria Town?**
Some large schemes are governed by their own laws or sit across jurisdictions. Check with the relevant authority and the scheme's own office.
